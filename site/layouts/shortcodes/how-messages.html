How a message moves

If {{ $call }} is down, messages move by the alternate repeater, simplex (radio to radio), HF radio or Winlink.

Forms

ARES and ACS use standardized FEMA forms for interoperability with the Incident Command System, used in conjunction with supported agencies.

Winlink

Winlink is email by radio, with forms and attachments. It works with or without the internet. State and USGS forms are built in; reports can be plotted on a map.
