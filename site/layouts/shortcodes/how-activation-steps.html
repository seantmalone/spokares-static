When the county calls

The county activates members; members also serve events and the National Weather Service without a county activation. ARES and ACS

It has happened for real: in the Gray and Oregon Road fires of August 2023, the group provided a mobile network unit for the incident command post and the Disaster Assistance Center.

  1. Request. The county needs radio support.
  2. Activation message. It goes to members registered for notices.
  3. Check in. Available members check in with net control on {{ $call }}.
  4. Assignment. Go to your assignment or a staging area.
  5. Support. Provide communications support to critical functions.
  6. Release. Served agencies and the emergency coordinator release members.
  7. Review. Once every station is secured and home, leaders hold an action review within 72 hours.
