How it works

It’s 2 AM.
The power’s out, and so is cell service.

  1. The phones go quiet. Ice brings the lines down overnight. At a warming shelter, a resident’s home oxygen concentrator has no power.
  2. A volunteer picks up the radio. The shelter’s ACS operator receives an ICS-213 message form and reads it, word for word, to net control on {{ $call }}.
  3. The radio room copies it. Another volunteer copies the message and hands it to the county’s Emergency Operations Center.
  4. The EOC acts on it. The request moves through the agency’s own channels. The necessary supplies are received. Crisis averted.

Radio still works, because people practice.
