ACS (optional)

  1. Join ARES.
  2. Submit the county’s Volunteer Emergency Worker Application (opens spokanecounty.gov).
  3. Pass the Sheriff’s Office background check. The county assigns your volunteer number.
  4. Attend New Member Orientation. It is held regularly; ask at a meeting or on the net for the next date.
  5. Serve as a New Member, normally up to a year, while you finish Level 1.
  6. Advance to Radio Operator and receive your ACS ID card.
