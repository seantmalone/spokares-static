ACS (optional)

  1. Join ARES.
  2. Complete the county’s Volunteer Emergency Worker Application.
{{ partial "ext-link.html" (dict "url" "https://www.spokanecounty.gov/FormCenter/Emergency-Management-31/Volunteer-Emergency-Worker-Application-276" "text" "Get the county application" "opens" "spokanecounty.gov" "class" "btn btn--red") }}
  1. Pass the Sheriff’s Office background check. The county assigns your volunteer number.
  2. Attend New Member Orientation. It is held regularly; ask at a meeting or on the net for the next date.
  3. Track your training in the ACS task book.
  4. Serve as a New Member, normally up to a year.
  5. Advance to Radio Operator and receive your ACS ID card.
